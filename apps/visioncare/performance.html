01 / BEFORE & AFTER

Less error.
More intention.

Team-reported testing found error fell from approximately 23–25% to below 1% after changes to delays, card sizing, and other interaction settings.

About these results

Source: VividVision team-reported before-and-after testing. The metric definition, participant count, trial count, testing conditions, and raw measurements have not been included on this page. These figures describe the team’s reported tests, not an independent clinical study or a comparison against Tobii Dynavox. Performance can vary by user and setup.
